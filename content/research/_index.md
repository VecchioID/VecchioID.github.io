<div class="research-grid">
  <article class="panel">
    <span class="kicker">01 · Learning</span>
    <h2>Representation Learning & Visual Reasoning</h2>
    <p>How can a model learn representations that expose compositional structure, abstract relations, and reusable reasoning primitives rather than only task-specific correlations?</p>
    <p>My work has focused particularly on Raven-style visual abstract reasoning and structured representation learning.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="panel">
    <span class="kicker">02 · Intelligence</span>
    <h2>Brain-inspired Intelligence</h2>
    <p>I am interested in computational principles inspired by biological intelligence: structured information processing, neural representations, and mechanisms connecting perception, reasoning, and decision-making.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="panel">
    <span class="kicker">03 · Systems</span>
    <h2>Efficient AI Systems</h2>
    <p>Recent engineering work extends from algorithms into the systems stack: large-model training and inference, distributed optimization, GPU/NPU profiling, memory and compute bottlenecks, and kernel-level performance optimization.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="panel">
    <span class="kicker">04 · Current direction</span>
    <h2>Learning × Systems</h2>
    <p>A longer-term goal is to connect better representations with efficient execution: models that reason effectively while respecting real hardware, memory, latency, and deployment constraints.</p>
  </article>
</div>
